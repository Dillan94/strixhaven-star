First-Years Make History: Better Spiritsong Clan Crowned in Inaugural Culinary Championship

By Mina Lee, Investigative Reporter

Strixhaven Stadium has hosted any number of wild events, Races, Weight-lifting competitions, Mage Tower finals, and at least one elemental storm, but never, in seven centuries, an event conceived, planned, staffed, and executed entirely by first-year students. That changed this term, as the first-ever Strixhaven Culinary Championship concluded before a massive crowd, with the Better Spiritsong Clan representing the Firejolt Café: Freshman, Daydream at Night "Dream", Freshman, Elidyr of the Spirit Song Clan "Ellie", and graduating Senior, Melting of Snow "Snow", taking the inaugural crown with a commanding 36 out of a possible total of 38 points!

The championship ran three relay-style rounds across the term, each built around a secret ingredient revealed only at the starting bell: whispering mushrooms that murmured preparation hints to those patient enough to listen, bottled pixie laughter that reduced half the field to helpless giggling, and, for the finale, the silvery tail-hairs of a BLINK DOG!

Once again, thank you to the Fantastical Horticulture Club for preparing the ingredients. Thrum Bouldereye Irondellve, Master of Grounds and Facilities, for the use of the Stadium. Freshman Zanther Bowen as the announcer. The Intramural Gymnastics Club for the opening ceremony. The Student-Mages of Faith for blessing the event. The Mage Tower Cheer Squad for cheering everyone on. The Playactors Drama Guild for the performances between rounds. And thanks to many others!

The winning finale dish was a croquembouche spun with caramel strands to mirror the creature's silver-blue coat!

The BiblioBuds, as the name suggests, represented the humble Biblioplex Café: Freshman Binx Brightwillow, Freshman Rosimyffenbip "Rosie" Wuzfeddlims, and Sophomore Drazhomir Yarnask answered with a laminated pastry praised by judges for flavors described as "genuinely complex," while guest competitor Marv of Brews and Stews presented a floating lava cake that this reporter can confirm smelled as good as it looked! The BiblioBuds and Marv of Brews and Stews tied for second place with 29 out of a possible 38 points!

In last place with a surprising 10 out of a possible 38 points, representing the Bow's End Tavern, the "Dream" Team brought the championship its most drama: head chef Curtie Axechucker, the tavern's famously unconventional kitchen master, flanked by the Spirit Song twins, Golden Bit "Bit" and Subtle Needle "Needle", juniors of Prismari and older siblings to winning-team captain Daydream at Night "Dream". The trio competed exactly the way you'd expect a tavern crew and two Prismari artists to compete: heavy on showmanship, illusion, and gamesmanship. Their tournament, however, ran unlucky from the start. Round one's deconstructed presentation left the judges' table cold and the team dead last on the scoreboard. Round two brought a spicy, effervescent cocktail that impressed on flavor but suffered mid-relay chaos, most memorably Needle spending a good portion of the round levitating twenty feet above his station. Then came the finale, an unfortunate disqualification as their round-three dish simply never reached the judges!

Judging duties fell to the Firejolt Café's manager Ellina Tanglewood, Professor Mavinda Sharpbeak, and Prismari's own Dean of Perfection, whose scoring was described by competitors as "exacting," "uncompromising," and "why is she looking at my plating like that?"

The road to the finale was not without incident. Round one saw a rat that witnesses claimed was named "Sec-RAT-ary" conducting what we have termed "unsanctioned inventory redistribution," while a goat identified as "Assistant Coach" consumed a meaningful percentage of the available ingredients on the "Dream" team's station before being escorted from the field by, shall we say, with mostly diplomatic means.

Champion and event organizer Daydream at Night "Dream" closed the ceremony not with victory remarks but with a handover. "This wasn't ours," she told the crowd. "It belongs to next year's first-years now. Make it better. Make it weirder."

Faculty observers note that the event has already been entered into the university's official records, meaning that, as of this term, the Culinary Championship — and any event it inspires — is not a stunt. It will hopefully become a new tradition. And as any Lorehold student will tell you, that is exactly how every tradition starts.
